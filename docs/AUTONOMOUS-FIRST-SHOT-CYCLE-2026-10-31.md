The operative rules are [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md), [worker contract](WORKER-OPERATING-CONTRACT-2026-10-31.md) and [private LAB scope](LAB-EXCLUSIVE-FAST-PATH-2026-10-31.md). This replaces the old serial Chief/Decision approval cycle. There are 15 existing ordinary OpenAI Scheduled roles; actual enabled/running state comes from official automation records.

## The next real result

1. Source/Identity pin a real private source snapshot, face and current-outfit facts. Renderer freezes the existing-model graph, seed, hypothesis and runnable script.
2. GPU Lab checks actual exclusive ownership, shared lock, queue, resources and protected data; then directly runs the bounded private diagnostic. Production HMAC/broker/merged combined source are not its prerequisites.
3. Producer inspects exact known output pixels. Golden independently compares originals/final RGBA with refs and mandatory facts in parallel and sends a concrete defect directly to its fixer.
4. The responsible stream owner chooses a causal change and the next bounded test. No Chief→Decision approval round is required. At most two diagnostics await independent review, and at most three GPU submissions belong to a hypothesis; both budgets survive worker/run changes.
5. Reliability/E2E measure and improve complete mandatory QA in the second stream. Pair timings from the same actual attempt; keep all mandatory checks and label partial measurements.
6. When quality and complete latency work together, freeze a new held-out background/sprites/three-CG pack before running it. Every case's first actual attempt counts. G2 and publication require independent authentic evidence; a diagnostic or retry is not a first-shot pass.

## Runtime limits

The existing legacy dispatcher keeps its declared prompt_text/native_pose factors and running/review_pending fences. If it cannot express SDXL, use the authorized separate private diagnostic runner with the same verified installed GPU lock, not a new root-derived lock. Never delete old fences, impersonate a reviewer or replay an unknown prompt. Known competing submitters are quiesced; restore prior services only after known completion and empty queue.

Unknown output, foreign owner/job, GPU fault or protected-state integrity failure stops affected runtime work. A known visual failure informs a new experiment; it does not freeze all development. No independent review may be fabricated and no candidate auto-published.

## Evidence that matters

For each experiment: actual prompt/output hashes, original-pixel observations, GPU and complete/partial timing, what changed and the next causal step. One private manifest plus a short redacted handoff is sufficient. The old frozen first-shot result remains historical; source merges, a minute timer's NO_APPROVED_WORK and uploaded model files do not demonstrate product progress.
